## Findings

## Overall

The later reports found real contradictions, but most of their fixes add machinery to the parts the initial zoom-out said to remove. Four simplifications resolve roughly 25 findings with less design: (1) n8n's own concurrency-1 queue instead of the processor; (2) guards removed at deploy time; (3) cut or flatten CUJ 5; (4) clear offered slots on release. Rerouting "close" and failure paths into handoff, and dropping CANCEL/END, take out most of the rest. Suggested order: spikes 1–3, then the simplifications, then any remaining critic findings. Verdict: needs revision, not rethinking.
